{{% ingredients %}}
- 1/2 cup peach preserves
- 1 TBSP balsamic vinegar of Modena
- 1 TBSP Dijon mustard
- 1 TBSP neutral oil
- 1/2 tsp dried rosemary or thyme (not both)
- Meat of choice (such as chicken or pork chops)
- Salt and pepper to taste (for meat)
{{% /ingredients %}}

{{% instructions %}}
- In a small bowl, whisk together peach preserves, balsamic vinegar, Dijon mustard, neutral oil, and rosemary or thyme. Reserve about 1/4 cup (or slightly less) in a clean bowl for serving.
- Pat meat dry with paper towels and season with salt and pepper.
- Begin grilling or searing meat. Flip and brush glaze over the surface; cook for 1-3 minutes, then flip and glaze the other side. Continue cooking and basting until meat is fully cooked through.
- Remove meat from heat, cover loosely, and let rest for 5 minutes.
- Brush with the reserved glaze and serve.
{{% /instructions %}}
