{{% ingredients %}}
#### Fish & Marinade
- 24 oz (6 filets) frozen tilapia
- 16 oz jar medium smoky chipotle tomatillo sauce (divided)
- 1/2 tsp salt

#### Peach / Pineapple Slaw
- 14 oz coleslaw mix
- 1/4 to 1/3 cup spicy southern coleslaw dressing
- 1 large peach, diced (or 1-2 cups diced pineapple)
- 2 TBSP lime juice
- 2 TBSP chopped cilantro
- 1/2 tsp salt

#### Serving
- 12 corn tortillas
- Extra cilantro for garnish
{{% /ingredients %}}

{{% instructions %}}
- Lay frozen fish filets in an even layer in a 9x13-inch baking dish. Pour 1 1/2 cups of the tomatillo sauce over the fish; reserve remaining sauce in the jar for serving. Cover and refrigerate for 4-6 hours to allow fish to thaw and marinate.
- Preheat air fryer to 400°F.
- In a large bowl, combine the coleslaw mix, coleslaw dressing, lime juice, chopped cilantro, and 1/2 tsp salt. Dice the peach or pineapple, add to the coleslaw mix, and toss until well combined.
- Remove fish from the marinade. Sprinkle the remaining 1/2 tsp salt evenly over the fish.
- Place fish filets in the air fryer basket in a single layer (work in batches if needed) and air fry for 10 minutes until flaky and cooked through.
- Heat a large skillet over medium-low heat and warm corn tortillas until soft and pliable.
- Gently flake the cooked fish into bite-size pieces.
- Assemble tacos by filling warm tortillas with fish and topping with the peach/pineapple slaw. Garnish with extra cilantro and reserved tomatillo sauce as desired.
{{% /instructions %}}
