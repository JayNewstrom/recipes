{{% ingredients %}}
#### Cake
- 1 3/4 cups + 1 TBSP (272 g) 1:1 gluten-free flour
- 1 3/4 cups + 2 TBSP (375 g) monk fruit sweetener
- Heaping 3/4 tsp baking soda
- 3/4 tsp salt
- 3/4 cup (180 ml) neutral oil
- 1 cup + 2 TBSP (270 ml) hot water
- 2/3 cup (63 g) unsweetened cocoa powder
- 3/4 cup (63 g) sour cream
- 3 large eggs, room temperature
- 2 tsp vanilla extract
- 3/4 cup mini chocolate chips

#### Glaze
- 4 oz bittersweet or semi-sweet chocolate, finely chopped (or chocolate chips)
- 1/2 cup (120 ml) non-dairy heavy whipping cream
- Mini chocolate chips for garnish
{{% /ingredients %}}

{{% instructions %}}
- Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease a bundt pan thoroughly with butter or oil (not cooking spray) and sprinkle with flour afterward, tapping out any excess.
- In a medium mixing bowl, whisk together the flour, monk fruit sweetener, baking soda, and salt. Set aside.
- In a large mixing bowl, combine the hot water, neutral oil, and cocoa powder; stir until smooth.
- Add the flour mixture into the cocoa mixture and stir until well combined.
- Mix in the sour cream, eggs, and vanilla extract until well combined.
- Fold in the mini chocolate chips.
- Pour batter into the prepared bundt pan. Bake for 55 minutes, checking at 50 minutes and tenting loosely with tin foil if the top is browning too quickly.
- Place on a wire rack for 5-10 minutes to cool before removing from the pan. Cool completely to room temperature before adding the glaze.
- For the glaze: finely chop the chocolate bar (or use chocolate chips). Place chocolate and cream into a microwave-safe dish and microwave at 50% power in 30-second increments until fully melted and smooth (about 1-2 minutes).
- Let the glaze cool slightly until warm (not piping hot). Pour or drizzle over the cooled cake and garnish with mini chocolate chips.
{{% /instructions %}}
