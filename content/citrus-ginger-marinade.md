{{% ingredients %}}
- 1 orange, zested and juiced
- 1 lime, zested and juiced
- 3 TBSP coconut aminos
- 1 TBSP monk fruit sweetener
- 1 TBSP minced ginger
- 1 TBSP honey or maple syrup
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 TBSP neutral oil
- 1 TBSP sesame oil
- 1 tsp cayenne (or preferred hot sauce)
- 2 lbs meat of choice (such as chicken)
{{% /ingredients %}}

{{% instructions %}}
- Whisk together the orange zest, orange juice, lime zest, lime juice, coconut aminos, monk fruit sweetener, minced ginger, honey (or maple syrup), minced garlic, neutral oil, sesame oil, and cayenne in a bowl.
- Transfer the marinade into a gallon zip-top bag and add meat.
- Seal the bag and marinate the meat in the refrigerator overnight, occasionally turning the bag for even coverage.
- Cook meat as desired (grilling, baking, or searing) until fully cooked through.
{{% /instructions %}}
