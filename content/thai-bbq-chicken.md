{{% ingredients %}}
#### Chicken & Marinade
- 1 lb (or slightly over) chicken breasts or thighs
- 1 TBSP minced lemongrass (or 1 stalk, white parts only, finely chopped)
- 1 Thai chili or jalapeño, deseeded and finely chopped
- 2 TBSP finely chopped coriander stems
- 5 cloves garlic, minced
- 1-2 tsp ground coriander
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp pepper
- 1 TBSP fish sauce
- 1 TBSP coconut aminos
- 1 TBSP sugar (or sugar alternative)
- 1 TBSP oil

#### Coconut Lime Rice
- 1 cup dry rice
- 3/4 cup lite coconut milk
- 3/4 cup chicken bone broth
- 1 tsp lime zest
- 1 TBSP lime juice

#### Thai Salad
- 3/4 cucumber, sliced into half moons
- 1 Thai chili or jalapeño, deseeded and finely chopped
- 300g cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1/4 to 1/2 red onion, thinly sliced
- 1/4 cup cilantro leaves, finely chopped
- 2-4 tsp fish sauce (to taste)
- 1-2 tsp sugar (or monk fruit sweetener)

#### Garnish
- Fried shallots
- Sweet Thai chili sauce
- Fresh cilantro
{{% /ingredients %}}

{{% instructions %}}
- In a bowl or zip-top bag, combine lemongrass, chili/jalapeño, coriander stems, garlic, ground coriander, turmeric, pepper, fish sauce, coconut aminos, sugar, and oil. Add chicken, toss to coat thoroughly, and marinate for at least 1 hour (or overnight).
- Rinse rice with cold water until water runs clear. In a pot or rice cooker, combine rinsed rice, coconut milk, chicken bone broth, and lime zest (holding back the lime juice). Cook until rice is tender and liquid is absorbed. Once done cooking, fluff with a fork, stir in lime juice, and cover to keep warm.
- Preheat air fryer to 390°F.
- Air fry marinated chicken for 14-16 minutes, turning halfway through, until golden and cooked through (internal temperature reaches 165°F). Let rest for 5 minutes, then slice.
- While chicken is cooking and resting, combine all salad ingredients in a bowl and toss well.
- Assemble bowls with coconut lime rice, sliced chicken, and Thai salad. Garnish as desired with fried shallots, a drizzle of sweet Thai chili sauce, and cilantro.
{{% /instructions %}}
