{{% ingredients %}}
#### Falafel
- 8 oz dried chickpeas (do not use canned)
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 cup tightly packed flat-leaf parsley, cilantro, or a combination
- 3 cloves garlic
- 3 scallions, white and green parts only, sliced
- 1 1/2 tsp ground cumin
- 1 1/2 tsp ground coriander
- 1/2 tsp cayenne
- 1/2 tsp baking powder
- Juice of 1/2 lemon
- Nonstick cooking spray

#### Wraps & Toppings
- 4 gluten-free pitas
- 8 TBSP hummus
- 1 on-the-vine tomato, thinly sliced
- 1 English or hothouse cucumber, sliced
- 8 oz tzatziki with feta crumbles
{{% /ingredients %}}

{{% instructions %}}
- In a large saucepan, combine dried chickpeas and 10 cups of water. Bring to a boil and boil for 2 minutes. Remove from heat, cover, and let sit for at least 1 hour, then drain thoroughly.
- In a food processor, add the soaked chickpeas, salt, parsley/cilantro, garlic, scallions, cumin, coriander, cayenne, baking powder, and lemon juice. Pulse for 30-60 seconds until chickpeas are finely minced but not pureed. Cover and refrigerate for at least 1 hour (up to overnight).
- Measure 3 TBSP of chickpea mixture and form into tight balls with hands to make 12-14 balls. Chill in the refrigerator for at least 30 minutes.
- Preheat air fryer to 400°F. Coat basket with nonstick cooking spray and place half of the falafel balls into the basket; spray tops with nonstick spray. Cook at 400°F for 8 minutes until browned and crisp, then flip, spray the other side, and cook for 6 minutes until evenly browned. Set aside and repeat with the remaining batch.
- While falafel balls are cooking, chop cucumber and slice tomato.
- In the last 1-2 minutes of cooking the second batch, warm or toast the pita bread.
- Assemble each wrap by spreading 1-2 TBSP hummus on a pita, layering with cucumber and tomato, placing 3-4 falafel balls, and topping with tzatziki and feta crumbles.
{{% /instructions %}}
